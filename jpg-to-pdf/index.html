Which page size to choose

Fit to image makes every page the same shape as its photo, with no white borders. It is the best choice for sharing photos or scans on screen.

Choose A4 or Letter when the PDF will be printed or uploaded to a form that expects standard pages. Each image is scaled to fit inside the margin and placed in the centre. Auto orientation turns the page to landscape for wide images.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn several photos into one PDF?

Add all the photos, put them in order and press Create PDF. Each photo becomes a page.

Will my photos lose quality?

JPG photos are placed in the PDF without recompression. Other formats are converted at high quality.

Why was my phone photo sideways in other tools?

Phones store rotation as a tag in the photo. This tool reads that tag and turns the page correctly.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The PDF is created in your browser.
