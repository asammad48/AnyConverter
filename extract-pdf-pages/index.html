Extract, split or remove?

Extract is for when you want a few pages and nothing else, such as one invoice from a statement. The original file is not changed.

If you want the whole PDF cut into parts, use Split PDF. If you want to keep almost everything and drop a few pages, Remove PDF Pages is quicker.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract one page from a PDF?

Click that page and press Extract. You get a one-page PDF.

Can I change the page order?

Yes. Turn on Keep the order I click and the pages are saved in the order you selected them.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Does the quality change?

No. Pages are copied without recompression.
