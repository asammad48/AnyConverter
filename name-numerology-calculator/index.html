How to calculate your name number

  1. Type your full birth name. Use the name on your birth certificate. Accents and letters like ñ, æ or ø are converted for you.
  2. Choose a system. Pythagorean is the most common. Chaldean uses different letter values and is popular in Indian numerology.
  3. Read the three numbers. Destiny uses every letter, Soul Urge the vowels and Personality the consonants. Every step is shown.

What this calculator does differently

  • Both systems, side by side — Switch between Pythagorean and Chaldean and see exactly which letter values change.
  • Every letter and every step — Each letter shows its value, and each total shows how it was reduced to a single digit.
  • Transparent accent handling — Accented and Nordic letters are converted to their base letter, and the page tells you which ones.
  • Compare two spellings — Check a nickname or married name next to your birth name.

How name numerology is calculated

Each letter of the alphabet is given a number. In the Pythagorean system the letters run 1 to 9 in order, so A=1, I=9, J=1 and so on. The Chaldean system uses older values from 1 to 8 and has no letter worth 9. Because the tables differ, the same name usually gives different numbers in each system.

The Destiny number (also called Expression) adds every letter in your name. Soul Urge adds only the vowels A, E, I, O and U, and Personality adds the consonants. Each total is reduced by adding its digits until one digit remains, except 11, 22 and 33, which are kept as master numbers if you leave that option on. Example: ANA in Pythagorean is 1+5+1 = 7.

Numerology is a symbolic tradition, not a science. Use the results for reflection and fun. Your name is processed in this browser tab and is never uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which name should I use? +
Use your full name as it appears on your birth certificate for a traditional reading. You can test nicknames or a married name separately with the compare field.
Pythagorean or Chaldean? +
They use different letter values, so the results can differ. Pythagorean is the most common in Western numerology; Chaldean is older and widely used in Indian numerology.
What is the Destiny number? +
It is calculated from every letter of your name and is also called the Expression number.
What is the Soul Urge number? +
It is traditionally calculated from the vowels in your name: A, E, I, O and U.
What is the Personality number? +
It is calculated from the consonants in your name.
Are 11, 22 and 33 reduced? +
No, not when “Keep master numbers” is on. They are shown as master numbers. Turn the option off to reduce them to 2, 4 and 6.
Do accents change the result? +
No. Accented letters are converted to their base letter (é → E, ñ → N, ø → O, æ → AE), and the calculator lists every conversion it made.
Is name numerology scientific? +
No. It is a traditional symbolic practice. Treat the numbers as entertainment and a prompt for reflection.
