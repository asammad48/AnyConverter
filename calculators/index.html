Which percentage calculator do I need?

Percent questions look alike but use different formulas. Use the percentage difference calculator to compare two values with no “before” and “after”, the price increase calculator when a price went up, and the reverse percentage calculator when you know the final amount and need the original.

For several discounts in a row, the discount stacking calculator shows why 20% plus 10% off is 28%, not 30%. For business pricing, the margin vs markup calculator keeps the two percentages apart.
