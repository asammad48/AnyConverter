How the birth card is calculated

A Tarot birth card is a Major Arcana card worked out from your birth date with a fixed formula. This calculator uses the method popularised by Tarot author Mary K. Greer: add the month, the day and the full year as numbers, then add the digits of the total until it is 22 or less.

If the result is between 10 and 21, it is your Personality card, and adding its two digits gives your Soul card. A result of 22 means The Fool (0) with The Emperor (4). If the result is 9 or less, the Personality and Soul card are the same. Example: 3 March 1985 → 3 + 3 + 1985 = 1991 → 1+9+9+1 = 20 → Judgement (XX) and 2 → The High Priestess (II). The only three-card case is 19: The Sun is the Personality card, The Magician (1) the Soul card, and Wheel of Fortune (10) sits between them as a hidden factor.

Some sites reduce the date digit by digit instead, which can give a different card. A birth card is symbolic, not a prediction, and your date never leaves your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Tarot birth card? +
A Major Arcana card derived from your birth date with a fixed formula. It does not change over time.
How is my card calculated? +
Month + day + year are added, then the digits of the total are added until it is 22 or less. All steps are shown under the result.
Why do some sites give two cards? +
Most dates give a pair: a Personality card (10–21) and a Soul card (its digits added). Different traditions also use different reduction rules.
Is this a tarot reading? +
No. It is a fixed calculation from your birth date, not a shuffled spread.
Does the card change? +
No. With the same formula and birth date you always get the same card.
Can I calculate someone else’s card? +
Yes, if you know their birth date. Please treat other people’s dates with care.
Is the birth date uploaded? +
No. It is calculated in your browser and not sent anywhere.
Is Tarot scientifically predictive? +
No. Tarot is a symbolic tradition, not a scientific method.
