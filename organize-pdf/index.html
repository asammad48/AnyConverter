What changes when you organize a PDF

Only the order and rotation of pages change. Text, images and links on each page stay exactly as they were, so quality is not affected.

The double-sided fix assumes you scanned every front page first and then turned the stack over, so the back pages came out in reverse. If your back pages came out in normal order, use the second option.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move a page in a PDF?

Drag it to its new place, or click it and use the left and right arrows.

Can I fix a double-sided scan?

Yes. Choose Fix double-sided scan and the fronts and backs are put back in the right order.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Does rotating here change the original file?

No. You get a new PDF and the original is left as it is.
