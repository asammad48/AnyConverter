How the score is calculated

The Instant mode uses the classic “LOVES” method from the school playground. Count how many times the letters L, O, V, E and S appear in both names together. That gives five numbers. Add each neighbouring pair and keep the last digit, which leaves four numbers, then three, then two. Those two digits are your percentage. Because only letter counts are used, swapping the names gives the same result.

Detailed mode takes 60% from the name score, 20% from how well your Sun-sign elements match (fire, earth, air, water) and 20% from how close your Life Path numbers are. Example: same element scores 90, fire with air or earth with water scores 80, other pairs 55.

It is a game. A low score says nothing about a real relationship. Names and birthdays are processed in your browser and never sent to analytics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Love Calculator work? +
It counts the letters L, O, V, E and S in both names and adds neighbouring numbers until two digits are left. Every step is shown under the result.
Will the same names get the same score? +
Yes, every time, and in either order.
Is the score scientifically accurate? +
No. It is entertainment based on letters, not a measure of a relationship.
Can I use birthdays too? +
Yes. Switch to Detailed mode to add star-sign elements and Life Path numbers.
Are names stored? +
No. They are only used in your browser and are never sent to analytics or a server.
Can I share my result? +
Yes. Download a share image, or copy a link that shows only your initials and the score.
Can I compare crushes or friends? +
Yes. Try as many pairs as you like. Just keep it light.
Does a low score mean a bad relationship? +
Absolutely not. It is a letter game.
