# Phase 07: Measurement, Experiments And Quarter Review

Timeline from report: Ongoing, with Week 12 review

## Objective

Measure whether the roadmap is working, run experiments for pages that have enough data, and decide the next quarter from evidence.

## Source Basis

Drawn from these report sections:

- Search Console checklist
- Analytics events
- Twelve-week roadmap, experiments and KPIs
- SEO and product experiments
- Final assessment and limitations

## Weekly Measurement

Every Monday, review:

- Indexed versus intended canonical URLs
- Crawled, currently not indexed
- Discovered, currently not indexed
- Duplicate without selected canonical
- Alternate page with proper canonical
- Soft 404s
- Sitemap status
- URL Inspection for homepage and representative cluster pages
- Performance clicks, impressions, CTR, and position
- Non-branded query growth
- Page-level impressions
- Country and language signals
- Device split
- Core Web Vitals
- Crawl statistics

## KPIs

Track weekly:

- Intended canonical pages
- Indexed canonical pages
- Indexed/submitted ratio
- Non-branded impressions
- Non-branded clicks
- Unique queries with impressions
- Queries in positions 1-10
- Queries in positions 11-20
- Median CTR by position
- Organic tool starts
- Tool completion rate
- Tool error rate
- Organic to download/copy rate
- Good CWV URL percentage
- LCP, INP, and CLS
- Referring domains
- Editorial links earned
- Spanish/non-English impressions
- Returning users
- Branded search impressions

## Experiments

Use multi-week experiments. Do not judge an SEO change after only seven days.

Priority experiment ideas from the report:

| Experiment | Control | Variant | Primary Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy title | Free Online X | X - Private, No Upload | GSC CTR |
| Intro copy | Generic description | Capability + format + privacy specificity | Tool starts |
| Example block | Instructions only | Real input/output example | Tool starts |
| Related tools | Bottom links only | Contextual workflow links | Internal clicks and impressions |
| Trust message | Generic privacy badge | Processed locally; network behavior documented | Completion rate |
| Troubleshooting | Generic FAQ | Real error/limitation documentation | Long-tail impressions |
| Category links | Generic directory | Cluster-specific contextual links | Crawl and impressions |
| Language page | Literal translation | Native search-intent copy | Local impressions and CTR |

## Week 12 Review

In Week 12:

1. Run a full regression crawl.
2. Review cannibalization and indexation.
3. Compare KPIs against Week 1 baseline.
4. Identify pages with impressions but weak CTR.
5. Identify pages in positions 11-20 for optimization.
6. Review product success metrics, not just traffic.
7. Decide next-quarter focus from data.

## Management Objectives

Use these as targets, not ranking promises:

- 100% of intended SEO URLs represented correctly in the sitemap.
- Zero accidental `noindex`, canonical, or redirect errors.
- More than 90% of genuinely intended pages indexed after adequate recrawl time.
- More than 90% of major templates meeting good CWV targets once field data is available.
- Tool errors trending down.
- Non-branded queries growing monthly.
- 10-20 legitimate, relevant referring domains earned rather than bought.
- Measurable impressions for several newly targeted long-tail tools.
- Clear GSC evidence about which country/language to scale.

## Checks

- SEO experiments are not too short to interpret.
- Crawlable content is consistent for users and Googlebot.
- Pages are not edited only to fake freshness.
- KPI review includes product quality, not just traffic.
- Next-quarter plan is based on GSC and analytics evidence.

## Deliverables

- Weekly KPI log.
- Experiment register.
- Week 12 crawl and indexation report.
- Cannibalization review.
- Next-quarter roadmap recommendation.

## Exit Criteria

This phase closes when:

- The Week 12 review is complete.
- Winning and losing experiments are documented.
- The next quarter's work is chosen from evidence, not volume-for-volume's sake.
