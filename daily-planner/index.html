Frequently Asked Questions

How does the daily planner save my tasks and schedule? +
Your daily schedule, time blocks, and task completion states are automatically saved directly in your web browser using local storage. Your planner data persists across page refreshes and browser restarts without requiring an account.
Can I view and schedule plans for past or future dates? +
Yes. Use the date picker at the top to navigate to any date. Each calendar day maintains an independent schedule and task list, allowing you to plan upcoming days or review completed routines.
What is time blocking and how do I use it in the planner? +
Time blocking is a productivity method where you divide your day into dedicated time slots for specific tasks. Enter start times and estimated durations to build a visual schedule that prevents multitasking and context switching.
Can I export or print my daily schedule? +
Yes. You can print your schedule or save it as a clean PDF document using your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P), formatted specifically for daily desk reference.
Is there a limit to how many tasks or time blocks I can add? +
No. You can add as many tasks and scheduled blocks as you need. Modern browser local storage easily accommodates thousands of entries with instant loading and zero lag.
