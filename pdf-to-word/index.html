Why PDF to Word is never perfect

A PDF records where each letter is placed on the page, not which words form a paragraph or heading. To create a Word file, the converter has to work out the reading order, columns, headings and tables again.

Choose editable text when you want to change the content: paragraphs flow and wrap naturally. Choose exact layout when the document must look identical, such as a form or flyer. If you cannot select text in the PDF, it is a scan, and OCR is needed to turn the image into words.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to convert confidential PDFs?

Yes. The file is processed in your browser and is never uploaded, so contracts, payslips and medical letters stay on your device.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Yes. Turn on text recognition and choose the document language. Scans take longer than digital PDFs.

Will the formatting be the same?

Exact layout keeps the look closest to the PDF. Editable text simplifies the layout so it is easier to edit.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes. Enter a page range such as 1–3, 7 before converting.

What about password-protected PDFs?

You will be asked for the password. It is used only in your browser to open the file.
