How the numbers are calculated

Your Life Path number is the most important number in Western numerology. Reduce the month, the day and the year of your birth to single digits separately, then add them and reduce again. For 15 July 1990: month 7, day 1+5 = 6, year 1+9+9+0 = 19 → 10 → 1, then 7+6+1 = 14 → 5. Reducing the parts first is the most common method and keeps master numbers that a straight digit sum can hide.

The Birthday number is simply the day of the month reduced. Your Personal Year adds your birth month and day to the current year and describes the theme of the year in numerology. When you add your name, the calculator also gives Destiny (all letters), Soul Urge (vowels) and Personality (consonants).

Different websites use different reduction rules, so results can vary. Numerology has no scientific support as a way to predict anything. The calculation runs entirely in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What numbers does this calculator calculate? +
Life Path, Birthday and Personal Year from your birth date, plus Destiny, Soul Urge and Personality when you add your name.
Do I need my birth time? +
No. Numerology uses only the calendar date.
Why do calculators give different results? +
They may reduce the full date at once instead of month, day and year separately, use a different letter system, or not keep master numbers.
What is my Life Path number? +
It is derived from your complete birth date and is seen as the main theme of your life in numerology.
What are master numbers? +
11, 22 and 33. Many traditions keep them instead of reducing them to 2, 4 and 6.
Can I calculate without a name? +
Yes. The date-based numbers work on their own.
Is my information uploaded? +
No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server or to analytics.
Is numerology predictive? +
No scientific evidence supports numerology as a way to predict events. Treat it as reflection and entertainment.
