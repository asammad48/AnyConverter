What to expect when you split a PDF

Pages are copied into the new files exactly as they are, so text, images and links stay the same. Splitting never lowers quality.

When you split by size, each part is kept under the limit you set where possible. A single page can be bigger than the limit, for example a high-resolution scan. In that case it gets its own file and is marked, and compressing the PDF first will help.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract only some pages?

Type just the pages you want, like 2–4, 9. Pages that are not in any range are left out.

Can I split a PDF to fit an email attachment limit?

Yes. Choose By file size and enter the limit, for example 10 MB for many email services. Each part is kept under that size.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The file is read and split in your browser, and nothing is sent to our servers.

Will splitting reduce the quality?

No. Pages are copied without being recompressed.

How do I save every page as its own PDF?

Choose One per page. You get a ZIP with one PDF for each page.
