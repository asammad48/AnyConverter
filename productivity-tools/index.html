Simple tools for everyday productivity

These free online productivity tools help with planning, timing, writing, organizing and quick choices. They are intentionally lightweight so you can open a tool, finish the task and move on.

Useful workflows

  • Plan work with a to-do list, sticky notes, Pomodoro timer or meeting timer.
  • Create small business assets such as invoices, privacy-policy drafts and QR codes.
  • Make quick choices with a coin flip, random picker, number generator or decision maker.
