Page numbers, page X of Y and Bates numbers

Simple numbers are fine for most documents. Page 3 of 12 helps readers notice if a page is missing, which is useful for contracts and printed handouts.

Bates numbers are used in legal work to give every page in a case a unique reference. They combine a prefix, often a client or case name, with a number padded to a fixed length, and they keep counting across documents when you set the start number.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start numbering on page 3?

Set First numbered page to 3. Set Start at to 1 if you want that page to show 1.

Can I add Page 1 of 10?

Yes. Choose the Page 1 of 10 format.

What is Bates numbering?

A legal numbering style with a prefix and fixed digits, like ABC-000123. Choose Bates and type your prefix.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
