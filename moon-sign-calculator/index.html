How to find your Moon sign

  1. Enter your birth date. Add the time if you know it. If not, tick “I don’t know my birth time”.
  2. Choose where you were born. Pick a city from the built-in list or enter coordinates. The time zone, including daylight saving, is applied for that date.
  3. Read the result. You get the sign and degree, or, without a time, either one certain sign or the two possible signs and the time the Moon changed.

Honest about what the data can tell you

  • Unknown birth time handled honestly — The whole day is checked. If the Moon changed sign that day, you see both signs and the exact switch time instead of a guess.
  • Exact degree — See the Moon’s position to the arc-minute, plus a warning if it sits right on a sign boundary.
  • Moon phase at birth — Phase name and how much of the Moon was lit.
  • No location lookup — Cities are built into the page, so your birthplace search never leaves the device.

How your Moon sign is calculated

Your Moon sign is the zodiac sign the Moon was passing through at the moment you were born. The calculator converts your local birth time to Universal Time using the time-zone rules in force on that date, then computes the Moon’s ecliptic longitude with the open-source Astronomy Engine ephemeris (based on the VSOP87 and ELP lunar theories, accurate to well under a hundredth of a degree). Each sign covers 30°: Aries 0–30°, Taurus 30–60° and so on.

The Moon moves about 13° a day, so it changes sign every two to three days. That is why the birth time often does not matter, but sometimes it does. Without a time we check the Moon at the start and end of your birth day: if both fall in the same sign the answer is certain; if not, you get both candidates and the local time of the change.

The astronomical position is measurable. The personality meanings attached to Moon signs come from astrology and are not scientifically established. Everything is calculated in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is my Moon sign? +
It is the zodiac sign the Moon occupied at the moment of your birth.
Do I need my birth time? +
Often not for the sign itself. It matters if the Moon changed sign on your birth day, and the calculator tells you when that is the case.
What if I don’t know my birth time? +
Tick “I don’t know my birth time”. You will see either one certain sign or two possible signs with the time the Moon switched.
Why is birthplace needed? +
It sets the time zone, including historical daylight saving rules, so your local birth time can be converted correctly.
Is Moon sign the same as zodiac sign? +
No. “Zodiac sign” or “star sign” usually means your Sun sign, which the calculator also shows.
How accurate is the result? +
The Moon’s position comes from the Astronomy Engine ephemeris and is accurate to a fraction of an arcminute. In practice the accuracy of your birth time and place is the limiting factor: the Moon moves about half a degree per hour.
Does the calculator store my birth details? +
No. Date, time and place are processed in your browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.
Is the interpretation scientific? +
The astronomical position is. The personality interpretation is astrology and is not scientifically established.
