Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my PDF say it has no interactive form fields? +
Not all PDF forms contain interactive digital form fields (AcroForms or XFA). If form blanks are simply static text or scanned images, fields cannot be automatically selected. For static documents, use a PDF annotation tool to overlay text.
What does flattening a PDF form mean? +
Flattening converts active interactive form fields (such as text boxes, checkboxes, and radio buttons) into permanent, uneditable page content. This ensures answers display identically across all PDF readers and cannot be altered.
Can I save my filled PDF form and edit it later? +
Yes. If you download your completed PDF without flattening, interactive form fields remain editable so you or a recipient can update values in any standard PDF viewer.
Are my filled form responses or uploaded files kept private? +
Yes. Form rendering, field population, and PDF export occur entirely inside your browser sandbox. Confidential personal or financial data entered into form fields is never transmitted to any external server.
Does this form filler support digital signatures and checkboxes? +
Yes. Standard interactive checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdown menus, and text fields are supported and can be clicked, typed, and saved with complete layout fidelity.
