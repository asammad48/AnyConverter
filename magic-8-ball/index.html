How to use the Magic 8 Ball

  1. Think of a yes-or-no question. Type it if you like. It stays on your device either way.
  2. Tap or shake. Tap the ball, press Space, or shake your phone after allowing motion access.
  3. Read the answer. One of the 20 classic answers appears in the window. Ask again as often as you want.

Small toy, done carefully

  • Shake your phone — Real shake detection on phones that allow motion access, with a tap fallback everywhere else.
  • The classic answer set — 10 yes, 5 maybe and 5 no answers, in natural English, Spanish and Danish.
  • Sound and motion you control — Turn sound on or off, and motion follows your reduced-motion setting.
  • Share the answer only — Share an image or text of the answer, with your question left out unless you choose to include it.

How the Magic 8 Ball works

The original Magic 8 Ball is a toy from the 1950s: a black ball filled with liquid and a 20-sided die printed with answers. When you turn it over, one face floats up to the window. Ten answers are positive, five are non-committal and five are negative.

This online version keeps the same 20 answers and the same odds. Each shake picks one at random using your browser’s secure random number generator, so every answer is equally likely and earlier answers have no effect on the next.

It is a game, not a fortune teller. Your question is never sent anywhere, and history is off unless you turn it on, in which case it is kept only until you close the tab.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Magic 8 Ball work? +
Each shake picks one of the 20 classic answers at random. Ten say yes, five say maybe and five say no.
Do I need to type my question? +
No. Typing it is optional. Just think of it and shake.
Can I shake my phone? +
Yes, on phones whose browser allows motion access. Tap “Enable shake” first if your phone asks for permission.
Are answers really predictive? +
No. It is entertainment, and every answer is equally likely.
Is my question stored? +
No. History is off by default. If you turn it on, it is kept in this tab only.
Can I turn off sound and motion? +
Yes. Sound is off by default, and motion can be switched off. Your device’s reduced-motion setting is respected.
Can I share an answer? +
Yes, as an image or text. Your question is only included if you choose to.
Is it free? +
Yes, free and with no sign-up.
