## Safety Rules

- Prioritize reputable directories, product communities, software catalogs, review sites, GitHub, and relevant editorial/community posts.
- Use mostly branded or naked URL anchors: `AnyConverter`, `AnyConverter free online tools`, `https://anyconverter.io/`.
- Use exact-match anchors sparingly and only when context is natural, such as `free online PDF tools` on a PDF tools listing.
- Avoid mass-submission services, auto-generated directory networks, paid link packages, comment spam, profile spam, link exchanges, private blog networks, and unrelated guest-post sites.
- Do not add sitewide footer/sidebar links on unrelated domains.
- Keep each listing unique enough to avoid duplicate-profile footprints.
- Track every submission URL so poor-quality links can be disavowed later if needed.
