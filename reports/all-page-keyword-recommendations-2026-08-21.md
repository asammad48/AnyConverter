## How To Use The CSV

Open `reports/all-page-keyword-recommendations-2026-08-21.csv` and sort by `priority`, then `gsc_impressions`. Start with P1 pages because they are either close to ranking, technically non-canonical, or have stronger immediate traffic potential. Query-to-page keyword matches are intentionally conservative; when the export did not prove a reliable query match, the report falls back to the page title/URL as the target keyword.
