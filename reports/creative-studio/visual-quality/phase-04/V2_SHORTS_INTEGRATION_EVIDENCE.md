# V2 short-form evidence

The 20-second study is an editable five-shot sequence. Project frame indices below are at 30 fps.

| Frames | Scene | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–134 | Five formats. One inbox. | Headline fade-rise, subject reveal, staggered entries, CSV underline |
| 135–254 | Special delivery | Title slide, parrot blink, envelope path and entrance |
| 255–374 | The seal is broken | Title spring scale, envelope rotation, anticipation, calm/recoil/shock, seeded camera settle |
| 375–479 | Keep the hall light on | Focus zoom and underline cue |
| 480–599 | Someone is waiting | Hard cut to the closer doorway, orbit, seeded camera settle and synchronized impact |

The atlas stores eight transparent cels in a body row and matching heads in a second row. Held pose IDs are mapped by the controller. The approved vocabulary comprises idle, blink, talk, shocked, excited, recoil, point and exit. The blink and shock strips bracket their actual changes frame by frame. The reaction preserves the perch anchor through the recoil and shock. The eye and neck anchors let head and gaze motion remain relative to the selected pose. Authored squash and stretch and small seeded camera movement account for the small pixel motion around the fixed perch.

The exported composition contains five actual captions and a 48 kHz stereo encoded soundtrack. The latter is recovered from four named timeline clips with starts at 0, 4.5, 12.5 and 16 seconds. The cue at the final cut is procedural sound design. No voice track is used in this caption-led film.
