## 2. Character & Vector Asset Architecture

### Original Character Staging (The Manager Parrot)
- **Character Design:** 100% original browser-native vector artwork rendered via HTML5 2D Canvas path primitives.
- **Pose States:**
  1. `neutral`: Resting folded wing, direct gaze into microphone.
  2. `wing-point`: Extended pointing wing gesturing towards the office agenda card.
  3. `shocked`: Dilated pupil, raised wings in surprise at "a meeting about meetings".
  4. `smug`: Confident posture, subtle head tilt, definitive comedic resolution.
- **Props:** Chrome desk microphone, executive acrylic nameplate, steaming coffee mug, office checklist with green checkmark on "NO MORE MEETINGS", red business necktie.
- **Dialogue Bubble:** Clean cartoon dialogue inside rounded speech bubble. All director notes (`directorNotes: 'Wing gesture towards the agenda.'`) are strictly separated and excluded from production renders.

### Minimal Noir Suspense Staging (The Last Signal)
- **Geometry & Architecture:** 1-point perspective corridor with slate-navy walls (`#151d2f` to `#0a101b`), vertical structural conduits, and recessed LED markers.
- **Volumetric Lighting:** Overhead industrial floodlamp cone (`rgba(254, 240, 138, 0.65)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.18)`), electrical light flicker dynamics, and illuminated dust motes.
- **Floor Reflections:** Wet polished floor tiles with high-contrast specular reflections (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12 - 0.42)`).
- **Silhouette:** Approaching trenchcoat & fedora detective silhouette with sharp 3.0px white/cyan rim lighting and glowing handheld datapad in hand.
- **Storytelling Narrative:** In-world narrative cues ("SECTOR 04 // VAULT CORRIDOR", "POWER GRID FAILURE", "TERMINAL INTERCEPTED", "EXTRACTION COMPLETE").
- **Luminance Improvement:** Pixels with luminance < 16 dropped to **0.0%–0.7%** (previously 94.7%–98.5%), providing readable mobile contrast.
