## 2. Qualitative Notes on Character Comedy (The Manager Parrot)

1. **Character Presence:** In the initial audit, the parrot character was small (occupying less than 15% of the frame) and felt disconnected from the desk. The corrected version scales the character up by 1.35x, anchors it behind the amber desk line, and gives it a chrome gooseneck microphone and meeting agenda.
2. **Pose Expressions:**
   - **Shot 1 (0-2.5s):** Neutral posture with bobbing head; opens meeting at microphone.
   - **Shot 2 (2.5-6.0s):** Wing-point gesture towards the meeting agenda board, pointing out items.
   - **Shot 3 (6.0-9.0s):** Shocked posture with wings flailed up when contradictor arrives, followed by smug satisfaction as the dialogue speech bubble pops up.
3. **Comedic Timing:** Synchronized bouncy C-major musical cadence with a comical pitch chirp at 5.0s.
