## Remaining issues and evidence gaps

### P1 — Audience-facing storyboard instructions leaked into actual exports
- At approximately the middle of the parrot pilot, the actual video shows **“Wing gesture towards the agenda.”** beneath the speech balloon. This is a staging instruction, not the cartoon character's dialogue or audience-facing narration. Remove such implementation/staging notes from all production renders.
- The noir pilot contains **“CREEPING SILHOUETTE”** and **“NEGATIVE SPACE HOLDS ANTICIPATION.”** The latter describes a filmmaking technique instead of delivering a suspense-story beat. Replace direction notes with actual narrative stakes, environmental clues and a clear reveal. Evidence: `Phase01_Current_Export_Visual_Samples.jpg` and original supplied PNGs.
- The SaaS master remains essentially a readable but visually repetitive sequence of dark technical cards, charts and headings. This is stronger than the engineering demo, but not convincingly comparable to high-quality Claude/Remotion ad compositions. Visual acceptance remains the owner's decision; no score generated by AGY establishes it.

### P1 — Extremely low noir image visibility
- Across five packaged noir samples at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, **94.7%–98.5%** of pixels have luminance < 16/255, with black median luminance. Suspense can be dark, but the subject and environment need readable silhouette separation, purposeful rim/edge light and at least one visual pay-off. Assess on normal phone brightness as well as a monitor; do not simply brighten everything indiscriminately.

### P2 — Negative gates do not test the real validation pipeline
- `test_negative_gates.js` uses five independent `assert()` statements on hard-coded constants (`2.515824`, `0.65`, `18.4`, `-91 dB`, a fake headline). Running it passes 5/5, but this demonstrates basic assertion behavior, not fault injection into the actual runner, export, audio analyzer, archive loader or parity parser.
- Replace with executable tests that perturb *actual fixtures* and invoke real gate functions. Assert the real process exits nonzero. Consider gate functions as shared reusable units rather than replicating assumptions.

### P2 — Missing end-to-end hard assertions
- Runner logs `freshCleanHeadline` but never asserts it equals `RAW DATA IS NOISY.`; an actual failed clean-state assumption could be missed.
- Runner records browser console errors but never asserts `results.consoleErrors.length === 0` before marking overall success.
- The test only checks video size and audio for the three source exports; independently counted frames are valid in this ZIP, but `ffprobe -count_frames` should become an automatic assertion inside the normal runner so future regressions fail immediately.
- The runner hardcodes a local Windows Chromium path; use standard Playwright executable discovery or a configurable `CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE_PATH`, and avoid assuming the host OS.

### P2 — Evidence and creative score overclaim
- The reports use an unreviewed **4.58/5.0** self-score and terms like “all defects fully resolved.” The audio being non-silent and the media being decodable does not establish professional storytelling or sound-design quality. Maintain separate technical and independent-human creative verdicts.
- The package contains source patches and evidence, not the complete runnable website code for the unpushed local feature branch. UI code evidence is credible, but the local browser flow was not independently replayed in this environment.
- The `PHASE_HANDOFF.md` still refers to the old correction runner in its re-run guidance. Update it to point at the authoritative `run_phase01_second_correction.js` and shared negative suite.
