## Visual review and limits

Independent review scored **85/100**. Narrative 18/20, composition 13/15, typography 14/15, motion 11/15, color 9/10, imagery 8/10, audio and captions 3/5, adaptation 5/5, and brand consistency 4/5. Remaining visual polish includes small product microcopy at phone scale and relatively static final holds. The illustrated dialogue remains caption-led with instrumental backing. The Phase 02 exports use built-in assets, and the reel is a separate externally composited project. Browser coverage is Chromium with desktop and emulated tablet/mobile viewports and touch. Physical Safari and mobile hardware were outside this run.

Application changes are limited to `assets/css/creative-studio.css`, `assets/js/creative-studio/studio-app.js` and the Creative Studio visual modules `creative-director.js`, `layout-composition.js`, `layout-editor.js` and `layout-solver.js`. The prior roadmap and Phase 17 work are preserved.
