Dialogue and suspense are 1280 × 720, 30 fps, 270 frames and 9.000 seconds. Product is 1280 × 720, 30 fps, 375 frames and 12.500 seconds. Both original and edited portrait files are 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 270 frames and 9.000 seconds. All clips use VP8 video and 48 kHz stereo Opus audio. [Metadata](evidence/dialogue-wide.ffprobe.json) · [Product metadata](evidence/product-wide.ffprobe.json) · [Portrait metadata](evidence/portrait-export.ffprobe.json)

The captions are stored as shot text with the shot's start frame and duration. Audible PCM backing clips are stored as local, deterministic sound recipes with gain and sample timing and encoded by the existing browser adapter. The parrot reuses an original Phase 01 Canvas drawing and pose definitions. The switch, shadow, calendar, report icons and illustrative UI were drawn in native Canvas with no downloaded asset bytes or new font dependency. The sample uses caption-led dialogue and existing instrumental backing. [Phase 01 provenance](../phase-01/VISUAL_ORIGIN_PROVENANCE.md) · [Phase 02 project](evidence/phase-02.acstudio)
