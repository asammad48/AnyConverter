### 7. What is the biggest confirmed reason rankings are weak?
**The Root Cause is a Two-Tier Failure:**
1. **The Primary Structural Blocker: Zero External Authority (DR ~ 0).**  
   AnyConverter is a 4-month-old domain with essentially zero referring domains (~2 verified profiles). In September, Google's algorithms gave AnyConverter a test exposure on Page 1 for `"ip address lookup"`. Because AnyConverter was competing against incumbent titans (NordVPN, WhatIsMyIPAddress) with 50,000+ referring domains, AnyConverter achieved only a **0.06% CTR** (28 clicks on 44,000 impressions). Google immediately demoted the page on October 6.
2. **The Secondary Technical Blocker: The Indexing Chasm.**  
   Over 50% of the site (including specialized tools like `camt053-to-csv/`, `barcode-generator/`, and `parquet-to-csv/`) are **completely unindexed (`URL is unknown to Google` or `Discovered - currently not indexed`)**. They cannot rank because Googlebot has not crawled them.
