- CTR: 0.09%
- Weighted average position: 66.71

This means the site appears in Google, but mostly around page 6-7 of search results. At those positions, traffic is expected to be extremely low.

## Why Views Are Low

The site has low views because:

1. Rankings are too low.
   Average position is 66.71, and most impressions are in positions 51-100.

2. CTR is extremely low.
   CTR is 0.09%, which is expected when pages rank far below page one.

3. Many pages are indexed but not trusted/ranked yet.
   Indexed pages increased to 322, but indexing alone does not produce traffic.

4. The strongest current opportunities are not the biggest English keywords.
   The data shows early opportunity in Spanish and Danish long-tail searches.

5. HTTP duplicate access still exists.
   HTTP URLs returning `200` can create duplicate URL signals, even with canonical tags.

6. There are still unresolved GSC coverage issues.
   32 pages are not indexed, including 2 not found URLs and 15 canonical alternatives.

7. The latest local SEO fixes need deployment.
   The live site should be updated so Google sees the improved metadata, schema, social tags, alt text, and CSP/security cleanup.
