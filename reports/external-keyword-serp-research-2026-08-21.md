# External Keyword and SERP Research - AnyConverter

Date: 2026-08-21

Scope: source-backed keyword and competitor research for the highest-opportunity AnyConverter pages found in Google Search Console and the all-page recommendation report.

Important note: this is external SERP research, not paid keyword-volume research. Exact monthly search volume and keyword difficulty require a connected keyword database such as Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, or Similarweb. The recommendations below combine:

- Google Search Console query/page data already exported for AnyConverter.
- Live web search results checked on 2026-08-21.
- Competitor page patterns, search intent, title/heading language, and content depth.

## Executive Finding

The fastest realistic SEO path is not to fight every high-authority English keyword head-on. The live SERPs show that broad English queries are dominated by old calculators, major PDF brands, ISPs, banks, and specialist utilities. AnyConverter should compete first where it has early GSC impressions and weaker SERP specialization:

1. Spanish PDF and utility long-tail pages.
2. Danish calculator/productivity/device-test pages.
3. English tool pages where privacy/browser-only positioning is credible.
4. Secondary keyword variants competitors mention but AnyConverter underuses.

## Competitor Patterns To Copy Carefully

### What strong competitors do well

- They put the exact query in the title/H1, then support it with variants in H2s and FAQs.
- They explain the workflow in 3-5 simple steps.
- They show concrete use cases instead of generic "fast and easy" copy.
- PDF competitors stress privacy, no signup, no installation, supported devices, and file limits.
- Calculator competitors include formulas, examples, reverse calculations, and common edge cases.
- Danish financial competitors build trust with loan assumptions, interest, term length, monthly payment, and comparison language.

### What AnyConverter can use as differentiation

- Browser-based processing where true.
- No signup.
- Simple interface.
- Lightweight alternative to heavy SaaS tools.
- Privacy-first wording for files that stay on device.
- Clear limitations so the page earns trust instead of sounding generic.

## Recommended On-Page Changes By Intent

### Calculator Pages

Use this structure:

1. H1 with primary keyword.
2. One short intro that repeats the exact phrase naturally.
3. Formula section.
4. Worked example.
5. Reverse calculation if relevant.
6. FAQ with 3-5 real questions.
7. Internal links to adjacent calculators.
