How to count down to a birthday

  1. Enter the birth date. Your own or someone else’s. The year is used for the age.
  2. Watch the countdown. Days, hours, minutes and seconds update live, in your own time zone.
  3. Save or add a reminder. Save the birthday on this device, or download a yearly calendar reminder.

More than a number of days

  • Live countdown — Days, hours, minutes and seconds until midnight on the birthday, in your local time.
  • Yearly calendar reminder — Download an .ics file that repeats every year in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
  • Leap-day birthdays — Choose whether 29 February is celebrated on 28 February or 1 March in other years.
  • Saved on this device — Keep a list of birthdays in your browser and add an optional photo that never leaves your device.

How the countdown works

The countdown finds the next date your birthday falls on, measured from midnight in your own time zone. If this year’s birthday has already passed, it moves to next year automatically, and on the day itself it shows a birthday message instead.

Alongside the countdown you see the age you are turning, the weekday of your next birthday, how many days you have been alive, your Sun sign and your birthstone. For a birthday on 29 February, you choose whether non-leap years count from 28 February or 1 March, since conventions differ.

Saved birthdays and photos are kept only in this browser’s storage. Nothing is uploaded, and birth dates are never sent to analytics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days until my birthday? +
Enter your birth date and the counter shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds until the next one, in your local time zone.
What happens after my birthday passes? +
The countdown automatically moves to the same date next year.
Does it update live? +
Yes, every second.
Can I add it to my calendar? +
Yes. Download the .ics reminder; it repeats every year in most calendar apps.
Can I save birthdays? +
Yes. Saved birthdays are kept in your browser on this device only.
What about February 29 birthdays? +
Choose whether to celebrate on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years. The countdown and calendar reminder follow your choice.
Can I use a photo? +
Yes, optionally. The photo is shown on this page only and never uploaded.
Is my birth date private? +
Yes. It stays in your browser and is never sent to analytics or a server.
