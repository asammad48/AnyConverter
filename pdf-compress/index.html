How PDF compression works

Most of a PDF’s size comes from images, especially scanned pages and photos. Light mode removes unused data and repacks the file, so text stays sharp and selectable, but the saving is usually small.

Balanced, Strong and target-size modes redraw every page as a compressed image. This can make a scan many times smaller, but the text can no longer be selected or searched. For forms you need to sign and send, that is usually fine. For documents you will edit, use Light.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a PDF to under 2 MB?

Choose Target size and enter 2. The tool tries different quality levels and keeps the best one that fits.

Will the text still be selectable?

In Light mode, yes. In the stronger modes, pages become images, so text can be read but not selected or searched.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The PDF is compressed in your browser and never leaves your device.

Why did my PDF hardly get smaller?

PDFs made of text and vector graphics are already compact. Big savings come from scans and photos.

Can I compress a password-protected PDF?

Not yet. Unlock it first, then compress it.
